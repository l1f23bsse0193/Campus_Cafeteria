How to Order

Four simple steps between your hunger and your hot meal. Follow them and you will be back in class before the bell rings.

🛒 Order in 4 Easy Steps

The same simple routine for every meal, whether it is breakfast or a late lunch.

🍽️
Step 1

Choose Your Items

Check the menu page or the board at the counter and decide what you want. Look at the deals page too, a combo can save you money.

Tip: decide before you reach the counter
💳
Step 2

Pay at the Counter

Tell the cashier your order and pay by cash or card. Prices are in PKR, and you will get a receipt for your order.

Show your student ID for deals
🎫
Step 3

Collect Your Token

After payment you will receive an order token with a number on it. Keep it safe until your food is ready.

No token, no pick-up
🥡
Step 4

Pick Up Your Food

Wait near the pick-up window. When your token number is called, hand over the token and collect your order.

Enjoy your meal!

📋 Good to Know

A few simple rules that keep the counter moving for everyone.

⏰

Peak Hours

The cafeteria is busiest from 12:30 PM to 2:00 PM. Order a little earlier or later to avoid the rush.

Quietest time: 3:00 PM onwards
📌

Pick-up Rules

Orders are held for 15 minutes after your token is called. Please collect your own order and show your token.

Uncollected orders may be given away
👥

Group & Bulk Orders

Ordering for a society event or a big group? Call the counter desk in advance so we can prepare everything on time.

Call before 6:00 PM
🙏

Keep It Clean

Please return trays to the trolley and keep the seating area tidy so the next student has a clean table.

Thank you for your help
